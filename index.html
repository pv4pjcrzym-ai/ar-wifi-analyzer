What you need

AR Wifi Analyzer uses the LiDAR scanner to build a 3D model of your rooms, so it needs an iPhone or iPad that has one. That's iPhone 12 Pro and later Pro models, and iPad Pro from 2020 onward.

Your device must be connected to the Wi‑Fi network you want to measure.

What the colors mean

While you walk, the app keeps measuring how quickly and reliably your router answers. Each spot is then colored on the walls and floor:

  • StrongThe router answers fast and without loss. Good for video calls and streaming.
  • FairWorks, but not reliable for video.
  • WeakSlow or dropping. Calls will struggle here.

Getting a good scan

  1. Start in a well‑lit room and hold the device at chest height with the camera facing forward.
  2. Walk slowly and steadily. Pan across walls and into corners so the scanner can see them.
  3. Cover every room you care about, including the places where Wi‑Fi usually drops.
  4. Scan one floor at a time and give each scan a clear name, like "Ground floor".

Questions

Does the app measure my internet speed?

No. It measures the connection between your device and your router – how fast it answers and how reliable the link is. That's what decides whether a room has good Wi‑Fi. A slow internet plan will not show up as weak coverage.

Why does the app need the camera?

The camera and LiDAR scanner are used to build the 3D model of your rooms and place measurements where you stand. Nothing is recorded or saved as video.

Live AR walkthrough doesn't show my coverage

Go back to the spot where you started the original scan and look around slowly. Once the app recognises the room, the saved coverage appears on the real walls. Good lighting helps, and moved furniture can make recognition slower.

The 3D model has holes or missing walls

Areas the scanner didn't see clearly are left out. Walk closer to those walls and pan slowly, or start a new scan. Mirrors, windows and very dark surfaces are hard for LiDAR to read.

Where is my data stored?

Everything stays on your device. Scans, 3D geometry and measurements are saved in the app's own storage and are never uploaded. Deleting the app deletes all scans.

How do I delete a scan?

Open the scan, tap the ••• button in the top corner and choose delete.
